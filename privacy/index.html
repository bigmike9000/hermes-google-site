Hermes Personal Assistant

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026.

This policy covers Michael Fang's private installation of Hermes Personal Assistant and its Google account connections. It does not describe every installation of the open-source Hermes Agent project.

Information accessed

Depending on the permissions granted to a particular connection and the task requested, the assistant can access account identity, contact information, Gmail messages and attachments, Calendar events, Tasks, and files in Drive, Docs, and Sheets. Gmail access is read-only.

How information is used

Google account information is used to carry out Michael's requests, including finding and summarizing information, preparing drafts, managing schedules and tasks, and working with documents. OAuth credentials are used to maintain the authorized connections.

Storage and service providers

OAuth credentials are stored on the machine running the assistant. Retrieved information can also appear in local conversation history, logs, saved files, and working caches. Relevant content may be sent to the AI model providers and other connected services configured for a requested task. Processing is therefore not exclusively on-device. Those providers may retain processing records under their applicable terms and settings.

This installation does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Transfers of Google user data are limited to what is necessary to provide the requested functionality, comply with applicable law, or protect security, consistent with Google's Limited Use requirements.

Hermes Personal Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

There is no single automatic deletion period for all local records. Conversation history, saved documents, and other retained copies remain until removed by the operator or the applicable storage cleanup process. Revoking Google access stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete previously retained copies. Michael can remove the local credentials and retained records and request deletion from a processing provider where applicable.

Control over access

Michael can revoke access through his Google Account connections. A new authorization may be required after revocation or a change in permissions. Other Google accounts are not invited to use this installation.

This informational website

The published site contains static informational pages. It has no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, or Google account data. The hosting provider may process standard web request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site.

Contact and changes

For questions or deletion requests, contact Michael Fang at lfang.michael@gmail.com. This page will be updated if the installation's data practices change.

Operated by Michael Fang. Private installation; no public sign-up.
